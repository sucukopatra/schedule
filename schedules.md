# Three weekly schedules

Written 2026-09-28. **B was picked and built the same day.** A and C stay here
for reference, in case B doesn't hold up.

## Same in all three

**Fixed, untouched:** every class, both labs on hold (PHYS 101 lab Mon
11:00–13:00, CHEM 110 lab Tue 14:00–16:00), Wednesday's Spare morning (C
shortens it, see there), Reading before bed every day 22:30–23:00, the Sunday
weekly review 14:00–15:30, Sunday chess games 19:30–21:00, and the Saturday
free evening 19:00–22:30. The trip weekend (leave Fri 19:30, back Sun 20:30)
stays as it is.

The classes, for reference:

| Day | Classes                                           | Free after |
|-----|---------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Mon | 09:00–13:00, 14:00–19:00                          | 19:00      |
| Tue | 09:00–16:00, no break                             | 16:00      |
| Wed | Spare morning 09:00–11:30, 12:00–14:00, 16:00–19:00 | 19:00    |
| Thu | 09:00–13:00, 14:00–16:00                          | 16:00      |
| Fri | 10:00–12:00, 14:00–16:00                          | 16:00      |

**Anki, every day, 15 min, at breakfast.** Weekdays 07:45–08:00, weekends
09:00–09:15. It keeps the old German block's start times, so this week's German
ticks carry over. It stays on during trip weekends too, since it's just your
phone.

**Meters.** Each thing gets its own meter:
- **German**: Anki, 7 a week. It keeps the old meter's history.
- **Nicos Weg**: new, 3 a week (2 in trip weeks).
- **Reading**: the nightly one, 7 a week, as now.
- **Long read**: new, 1 a week (none in trip weeks).

**Gym: 5x5, three days a week, 75 min, never two days in a row.** 5x5 needs
a rest day between sessions, and all three alternatives give it one. The gym
targets don't need to change: normal 3, trip 2, exam 2, with a spare session
in exam weeks.

**Deep sessions: none at weekends.** Normal and exam weeks need 6 and trip
weeks need 4. With Saturday gone, the easy weekday slots only add up to five:
Tue 16:00, Wed 14:00, Thu 16:00, Fri 12:30, and the newly free Fri 16:00. The
sixth has to come from somewhere, and **that's the main difference between
the three**.

**Meal prep** is 2.5 h at Baki's, twice a week, with one big batch in trip
weeks. Baki can move the days (confirmed 2026-09-28), so meal prep goes
wherever it fits best.

Tuesday's meal prep moves to Sunday.

## B: Tue/Thu/Sat gym, long Friday

The gym comes straight after the Tuesday and Thursday deep sessions, which
gets you moving after a day of sitting. Friday becomes the long work day,
starting early, and then the weekend begins at 18:00. Monday evening, after
the longest day, stays almost empty.

| Tue | Anki 07:45 · classes to 16:00 · **Deep 16:00–18:00** · **Gym 18:30–19:45** |
| Wed | Anki 07:45 · Spare morning · MATH · **Deep 14:00–16:00** · GAME 209 · **Meal prep 19:30–22:00** |

  the three: Sunday cooks for Mon–Wed and Wednesday cooks for Thu–Sat.

## C: Morning gym, five deep sessions

The dorm gym makes mornings possible, so lifting happens before the day
starts and every evening stays open. Meal prep doesn't move. The price:
there are **5 deep sessions instead of 6**, so the targets come down.

| Day | Blocks |
|-----|--------|
| Mon | Anki 07:45 · classes to 19:00 · **Nicos Weg 20:00–20:30** |
| Tue | Anki 07:45 · classes to 16:00 · **Deep 16:00–18:00** · Meal prep 19:45–22:15 (unchanged) |
| Wed | Anki 07:45 · **Gym 09:00–10:15** · Spare morning 10:15–11:30 (shorter) · MATH · **Deep 14:00–16:00** · GAME 209 · **Nicos Weg 19:30–20:00** |
| Thu | Anki 07:45 · classes to 16:00 · **Deep 16:00–18:00** · Meal prep 18:30–21:00 (unchanged) |
| Fri | Anki 07:45 · **Gym 08:15–09:30** · lab · **Deep 12:30–14:00** · MATH · **Deep 16:00–18:00** · **Nicos Weg 18:15–18:45** |
| Sat | Anki 09:00 · nothing else · Free evening |
| Sun | Anki 09:00 · **Gym 10:00–11:15** · **Long read 11:30–13:00** · Review · Chess games |

- **Deep:** 5 slots. The targets would drop:
  - normal: coursework 3, software **1**, chess 1;
  - exam: coursework **5**;
  - trip: unchanged at 4, which leaves 1 spare slot.
  If you'd rather keep software at 2, the sixth session goes at
  Wed 20:15–21:45. Wednesday then gets long.
- **Heaviest day:** none stands out. Friday starts with the gym at 08:15,
  which leaves half an hour to shower before the 10:00 lab.
- **Trip weeks:** Sunday gym is off, so gym is Wed and Fri. All three Nicos
  Weg lessons are on weekdays, so trip weeks lose none.
- **Meal prep:** stays on Tue and Thu.

## Side by side

|                       | A: Mon/Wed/Fri        | B: Tue/Thu/Sat           | C: Morning gym            |
|-----------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Gym                   | Mon, Wed, Fri evening | Tue, Thu evening, Sat morning | Wed, Fri, Sun morning |
| Deep sessions         | 6, all weekdays       | 6, all weekdays          | 5, targets come down      |
| Where the 6th went    | Tue 19:00             | Fri 08:15                | Nowhere                   |
| Heaviest day          | Tue                   | Fri                      | None                      |
| Meal prep             | Thu + Sun             | Wed + Sun                | Tue + Thu (as now)        |
| Nicos Weg             | Wed, Fri, Sun         | Mon, Thu, Sat            | Mon, Wed, Fri             |
| Long read             | Sat morning           | Sun late morning         | Sun late morning          |
| Saturday              | Long read, then free  | Gym + lesson, then free  | Completely free           |

**My pick: B.** It keeps all 6 deep sessions without making Tuesday worse.
Tuesday is already the one day with no break at all. B also leaves Monday
evening free after the longest day, and lifting right after the Tue/Thu deep
sessions breaks up the sitting. Since Baki can move the meal prep days, B's
Wed/Sun meal prep costs nothing. C is the pick if you'd rather have fewer
deep sessions and every evening free.

Mixing is fine too, for example B's gym days with C's meal prep. Just tell me
which pieces you want.
